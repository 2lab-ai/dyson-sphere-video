# v4 plan — dopamine rhythm: pattern inside novelty (2026-10-07)

> **Canonical sections: R1 corrections C1–C5 and the table C6 at the end.** The draft sections P1–P4, options A/B/C and the first plate table are kept as history and are superseded wherever they differ. The numbers (9–10 ones, ≥120 px, 3+1) are creative rules, not research-proven constants; the research supports the shape (mostly-2, bursty 1s, denser later), not the numbers.

다른데, 1. 20%만 똑같고 80%가 다를경우랑 (좇나 생소한데 뭔가 패턴이 보이는 느낌) 2. 80%가 똑같고 20%가 다른경우 (좇나 편안한데
상큼하게 신선한 부분이 있음). 이 2가지 스타일을 적절히 섞어야함. 예를들어 음악의 경우는 첫 씬 -> 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 하다가
중간에 한번 1로 갔다가 1에서 2 2 22 2 1 222 1 1 22 1 22222 1 222 1 이런식으로 만들어야함. 누군가 글로 쓰거나 논문이 있을수도

## What the research says (scratchpad/novelty-research.md; URLs there)
- Closest formal statement = Cheung et al. 2019 (Current Biology): pleasure = uncertainty × surprise interaction. Two live cells:
  **low uncertainty + high surprise** (= owner's type 2) and **high uncertainty + low surprise** (= type 1). Two dead cells:
  all-familiar and all-new. Gold et al. 2019 (J Neurosci) replicates on melodies. Nobody publishes 80/20; the inverted-U is
  everywhere (Berlyne, Kidd 2012, Witek 2014, Bornstein 1989, Hekkert 2003 MAYA); the numbers are the owner's.
- Dopamine is prediction-error (Schultz 1997) and fires in *anticipation* (Salimpoor 2011) — the dopamine mode is type 2
  built up, then paid off; type 1 alone is the least pleasant cell. Repetition is itself the liking engine with a satiation
  ceiling (Nunes 2015: each extra chorus repeat +14.5 % odds of #1; Margulis 2014) — which is why 1s are mandatory.
- Sequence: Huron 2006 (contrastive valence) and Schmidhuber (compression progress → 1s denser later) support the owner's
  shape; Cutting 2010/2011 supports bursty non-periodic spacing with occasional doublets and longer section-opening plates.
- A type-1 plate still needs a plain anchor (Hekkert: typicality must survive); breaking the anchor = chaos, not type 1.

## v4 principles (binding)

### P1 — The spine (100 % constant, every plate)
1. **Lyric grammar:** Black Han Sans, ≥120 px cap-height at 1080p (≥140 px on home plates), white or the plate's `text`
   token, enters on the beat of its first syllable, one line on screen at a time, sung syllable in signal orange. The
   idiom may *materialise* it (LED, neon, stitched…) but never below 120 px and never re-flowed into a new grammar.
2. **The staged circle:** the signal-orange point / disc (sun, eye, moon, clock, pupil) sits at the storyboard anchor
   (1187, 413) whenever it is on screen, at one of three sizes (point 12 px · disc 110 px · full 420 px). It is the
   through-line: on home plates it is literally the same disc.
3. **The beat kind:** every beat = one visible event of the same *kind* across the film: a **snap** (a hard, 1–2-frame
   change of a structural element) — never a smooth pulse. Every downbeat = a **structural hit**: cut-in ≥1.5× or a
   2-frame full-frame flash or a ≥20 px frame jolt. Section hits (65.1, 142.0, 165.3) = whiteout flash + cut-in.
4. **The colour thread:** signal orange `#FF5A14` is present on every plate (the circle, the sung syllable, or both).

### P2 — Types, operationally (relative to what the viewer has already seen in THIS film)
Dimensions: {medium/world, palette+ground, camera class, layout, subject}. The spine is outside these.
- **Type 2:** ≤1 dimension changes vs the previous plate, and the change is a *finer* detail than the last change
  (new subject in the same world; new angle in the same camera class; one new accent). Everything else continues.
- **Type 1:** ≥4 dimensions change at once; the spine survives untouched and is made *plainer* than usual (bigger lyric,
  the circle present and still, the hit on the downbeat only).
- **Within every plate (3+1):** beats 1–3 repeat the plate's snap; beat 4 deviates (skip, double, colour, direction). On
  plates longer than 2 bars the camera jumps every bar; on 4-bar plates bar 4 is the deviation bar.
- Durations: section-opening plates are the longest in their section (3–4 bars); mid-section plates 1–2 bars.

### P4 — Intensity floor (every plate, both options)
- Replace the T1/T2 caps: downbeat cut-in 1.5–2.5× (hard, not eased) or a 2-frame flash (bone or the plate's `hi`) or a
  20–30 px jolt; per-beat snap must move ≥5 % of frame area or ≥8/255 mean luminance.
- Lyric ≥120 px everywhere (fix p08 p10 p12 p20 p23 p30 at ~40–60 px). No plate holds one composition > 1 bar without a
  snap; p45 static hold removed (the point steps, the hairline undraws per beat — already in storyboard, not in render).
- Weak downbeats p06 p10 p14 p30 p32 get a structural hit.
- Gate (new, render-based): `scripts/hit-gate.py` samples the rendered video at 60 fps around every downbeat and fails if
  the frame-difference spike is < threshold on any downbeat, and fails any ≥1-bar window with no snap. A `nov` field
  (`1|2|R`) per plate in edit.json and a `T.rhythm` check that the string equals P3 and that type-2 plates share ≥4
  dimensions with their predecessor (dimensions declared per plate: world, palette, camera, layout, subject).

### Option B — Grammar home (80/20 by grammar, worlds stay diverse)
Keep v3's 35 worlds (last week's requirement) but make the *grammar* the 80 %: inside a 2-run every plate shares ground
brightness, palette family (one hue family + orange), camera class, lyric layout and hit kind; only the medium/subject
changes. Ones break ≥4 of those. Cheaper (retouch, no new variants) but the "comfortable" feeling rests on grammar alone;
the medium still changes every plate, which the review found reads as type 1 on its own.

### Option C — Hybrid (research §4 + review §8)
Four cross-cutting **home families**, staged identically every time they return (spark point / cosmos sun / pop-up paper /
8-bit), each returning every 3–5 plates with one twist (= the 2s), and ~15 standalone v3 idioms kept as the 1s (p02 p05
p07 p11 p21 p24 p25 p26 p33 p34 p35 p36 p37 p40 p44). The string becomes 2-heavy because the homes recur; the 1s are the
best v3 plates. Cost: ~12 new home variants; ~14 v3 idioms dropped.

## C2 — What "2" means here (settles A vs C)
2 = continuity with the **previous** plate (prediction forms from the last moment): ≥4 of {world/medium, palette+ground,
camera class, layout, subject} persist, exactly one changes, and the viewer can name what persisted. A run is 2–6
consecutive 2s in one world. "Home" is not a type: a callback (p29, p45) is a 2 against its `ref`. The rule "each change
finer than the last" is dropped (not required, not supported).

## C4 — Lyrics: no karaoke
Applies only to plates with lines. One glyph set (Black Han Sans outlines), ≥120 px projected height, enters on its first
syllable's beat, **stamps** the sung syllable in orange on its own beat and leaves it (never a running fill). The idiom may
light/print/occlude/texture the glyphs but may not re-flow them. Instrumental plates carry no text; their 20 % is the circle
(≥110 px when it is the anchor; 12 px only as a trace) and the snap.

## C5 — Hit hierarchy and gates
- Every beat: a snap whose result **persists** ≥6 frames (a door opens, a panel turns, a row flips — not a flash).
- Downbeat: a bar accent (cut-in 1.5–2.5× hard, or a ≥20 px jolt, plus the snap).
- Whiteout: only 65.120, 142.035, 165.343 and p37.
- `hit-gate.py` (render-based): for every beat, frame Δ at the beat ≥ 5× that plate's 60-fps median Δ AND (Δ mean lum ≥ 8/255
  OR changed area ≥ 5 %), and the change persists ≥6 frames; also visible at 4 fps. Thresholds calibrated on v3's known weak
  (p06 p10 p14 p30 p32) and strong (p13 p25 p37 p40) plates before use. Photosensitivity: ≤3 full-frame flashes/s over any
  1 s window including cuts.
- `nov` field + `ref` per plate = lint (T.rhythm: string equals C1). Pixel check for consecutive 2s: same ground class and
  |Δ mean lum| < 40 at the cut (reuse measure.py).
- Lyric minimum enforced in the single `drawLyric` entry point (min 120 px), not by a scan.
- Gate constants to change: MAX_DARK_RUN 4 → 7 (verse1 run is dark ×7 by design; pre1 is light ×5); FAMILY_WINDOW_MAX 3 → 6;
  IDIOM_REPEAT allow-list replaced by `run` membership (same world allowed inside a declared run, max 6).
